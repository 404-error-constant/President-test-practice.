🏛️ U.S. Presidential Party Affiliations

A breakdown of the political parties that have shaped the American executive branch from 1789 to the present. I find it helps to remember the exact number of Democrats and Republicans, so you can check your test before you turn it in.

Political Party Presidents Historical Context
Republican 19 Includes Lincoln, T. Roosevelt, and Reagan.
Democratic 16 Includes FDR, JFK, and Obama.
Democratic-Republican 4 Dominant early 19th-century party.
Whig 4 Active from 1833 to 1854.
Federalist 1 The party of John Adams.
Independent / None 1 George Washington.

💡 Fun facts

  • The Washington Precedent: George Washington is the only president to never belong to a party; he actually warned against them in his final address.
  • Counting Method: These numbers refer to individual people. Note that Grover Cleveland is counted as one person, even though he served two non-consecutive terms. Same with Trump
  • Evolution: The Democratic-Republican party eventually split into the modern Democrats and the Whigs.
  • 2 of the 4 whigs did not finish their terms: William Henry Harrison (1841): Died 32 days into his term from pneumonia, believed to be brought on by giving a long inaugural address in cold weather without a coat. Zachary Taylor (1850): Died suddenly 16 months into his term, likely from some type of cholera after consuming raw fruit and milk at a Fourth of July celebration, a true patriot.
  • Brute force tactics: Simply memorize this number, it repersents the how many year each president served.
